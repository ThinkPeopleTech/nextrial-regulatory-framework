# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is an open standard developed in public through adversarial engagement. We
pledge to make participation in the project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

The framework improves by being challenged. Rigorous, specific, technical
disagreement is welcome and encouraged. Personal attacks are not.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Challenging a claim with evidence, a counter-example, or a regulatory citation.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive critique.
- Focusing on what is best for the standard and the patients it ultimately serves.
- Showing empathy and respect toward other community members.
- Acknowledging the contributions of others accurately and without overstating
  endorsement.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting another participant's position or contribution.
- Asserting or implying institutional or regulatory endorsement that has not been
  given. (See the contributors disclaimer: contributions are individual and do not
  imply institutional endorsement.)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and working sessions — and when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
to the project maintainer at **conduct@nextrial.ai**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.
